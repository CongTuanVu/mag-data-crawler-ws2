Aeropolis is the integrated and synergistic off-terminal real estate development and solutions by Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad with a focus on key economic growth activities aligned to national blueprints, concentrating on 3 core clusters at present namely Air Cargo and Logistics, Aerospace and Aviation, and MICE and Leisure.

With KLIA as the flagship development, complemented with the Subang Airport / Lapangan Terbang Sultan Abdul Aziz Shah (LTSAAS) Regeneration plan, Aeropolis aims to catalyse the aviation ecosystem and attract global operators in the eCommerce logistics and aerospace sectors, and ultimately elevate Malaysia as a magnet for foreign direct investments and as a high-income nation.

With limitless potential, Aeropolis is the answer for tomorrow’s world.

 

The Game Changer

KLIA Aeropolis is set to be a game changer in transforming the future of Malaysian aviation; leveraging on the Kuala Lumpur International Airport's available capacity to cater up to 140 million passengers per annum with a potential 4th runway.

At the same time, LTSAAS Regeneration is a comprehensive plan that leverages LTSAAS strategic location and builds on its recognition as a mature aerospace ecosystem, effectively transforming it into a global, best-in-class aerospace and business aviation hub
