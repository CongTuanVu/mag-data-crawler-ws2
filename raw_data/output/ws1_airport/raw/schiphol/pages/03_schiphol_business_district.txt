Schiphol Business District
Offices at a Prime Location
Discover a business hub that excels in national and international accessibility, strategically located near the terminal, train station, and highways A4, A5, A9, A10. Here, you'll find high-quality and sustainable office spaces with a view of the airport. The key to success lies in unparalleled accessibility through all modes of transportation (train, plane, bus, and car) and the rich array of facilities available.
At the heart of the Schiphol Business District is the World Trade Center Schiphol Airport, including the most sustainable building at Schiphol with a BREEAM In-Use Very Good label. Successful business, meetings, relaxation, and inspiration come together seamlessly here.
