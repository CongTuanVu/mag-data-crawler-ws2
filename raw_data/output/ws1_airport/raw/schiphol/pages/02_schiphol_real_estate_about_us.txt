About us
The most accessible hub location in the Netherlands
Schiphol is a unique location for establishing businesses, centrally located within the Netherlands and directly connected to the rest of the world. For over 100 years, this area has been a bustling and dynamic hub for both international and national companies. Currently, more than 450 companies are located here.
The buildings and areas surrounding the airport are owned by Royal Schiphol Group. Schiphol Real Estate plays a significant role in this context. We are not only the lessor of the 35 business buildings and offices but also serve as a dedicated area director. We ensure continuous development of the area and our buildings, offering the necessary flexibility as the needs of our tenants evolve. We provide a place where organizations prefer to establish themselves, where people meet, and where they enjoy working.
Innovative development and smart design
Schiphol Real Estate creates a work environment that facilitates, surprises, and inspires. We constantly engage with tenants so that we can actively respond to their needs. We achieve this by implementing smart and sustainable technologies that provide insights into usage so that tenants can make more efficient use of their space. Additionally, with these insights, we can manage our location more effectively. Our focus areas are flexibility, well-being & sustainability, connectivity, and inspiration.
Flexibility
Solutions that scale with you.
The adaptability of an organization is essential in today's world. Markets and needs change rapidly. Therefore, we consider it important to proactively adapt. As area director, we offer flexible real estate solutions, considering the needs of our tenants, even as the demand for available space changes. With our extensive portfolio of buildings, there's almost always a suitable solution available.
Discover our portfolio
Well-being and sustainability
Concern for people, business, and the planet.
We believe it is important for everyone to contribute to the balance between the well-being of businesses, employees, and the planet. Our sustainable goals guide how we manage our real estate and areas. The sustainability of our real estate portfolio is an ongoing process. We work hard to reduce energy usage and focus on circular construction methods. We also pay close attention to the well-being of all employees in our buildings by providing pleasant and productive workspaces.
More about our sustainable ambition
Connectivity
A community that fosters business and personal growth.
Schiphol connects. From connection arise new opportunities, insights, and partnerships. At the centre of this is our community platform Spot, which facilitates meetings, knowledge sharing, and personal development through events, workshops, sports activities, and gatherings. This creates an environment that encourages connection. An environment with a unique mix of companies and employees who meet and inspire each other.
Get to know your Spot community
Inspiration
An inspiring work environment that works for people.
Schiphol offers a dynamic work environment, buzzing with energy 24/7. It is the place where the world comes together. We understand that life and work are increasingly intertwined, and as a result, the demands of the work environment change. That is why various convenient amenities are within reach, such as childcare, supermarkets, coffeeshops, restaurants, and fitness clubs. Everything necessary to stimulate the productivity and creativity of people and businesses. Schiphol is an environment that inspires and works for today's employees.
