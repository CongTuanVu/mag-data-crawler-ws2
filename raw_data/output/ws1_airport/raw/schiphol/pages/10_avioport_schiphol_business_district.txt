Schiphol Business District
Avioport
A dynamic place to do business
Avioport, a multi-tenant building, is located in the Schiphol Business District (CBD) and prominently visible from the A4. This modern office building with an extensive entrance hall and beautiful glass lifts offers fantastic views over the international airport. This makes it a unique place for working and doing (international) business.
Just steps away is a wide range of comforts like a gym, restaurant, AH to go, childcare services, Starbucks, flex work spaces and meeting rooms. This prime location is perfectly accessible by car, train, bicycle or bus. And of course, by air.
