Schiphol East
Offices with a unique character
An office space near the General Aviation terminal platform and within walking distance of the Amsterdamse Bos? Schiphol East is a dynamic location that is easily accessible from Amsterdam and surrounding areas.
International companies such as Perfetti van Melle, KLM, and Honeywell have established themselves here with satisfaction, as well as nationally operating companies like Skyjob and LVNL. The office buildings in Schiphol East each have a unique character. The modern office units can be flexibly arranged, starting from 50 m² to 1,000 m² rentable floor area.
