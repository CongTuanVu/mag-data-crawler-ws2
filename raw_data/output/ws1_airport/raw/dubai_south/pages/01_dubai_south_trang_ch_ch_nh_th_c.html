Work and lifestyle
solutions
LIVE

In Dubai South, you’ll find diverse residential and investment opportunities in a thriving community. Tradition meets modern living with amenities like green spaces, sports facilities, schools, and medical services. Various options cater to different budgets and lifestyles.

WORK

Conduct your business in the ideal place. Enjoy a diverse community, a Freezone with 100% foreign ownership, and flexible office solutions. It’s a strategic hub for efficient distribution and innovation, and home to the Mohammed bin Rashid Aerospace Hub.

THRIVE

Dubai South is where success thrives through innovation, technology, opportunities, sustainability, and smart investment. It offers an ideal hub for businesses with efficient operations, global access, skilled workforce, and eco-conscious practices. Explore endless possibilities in The City of You!
