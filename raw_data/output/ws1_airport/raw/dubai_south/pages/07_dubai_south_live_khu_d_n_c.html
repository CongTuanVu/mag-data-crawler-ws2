This dynamic community features a range of freehold and leasehold residential projects, catering to diverse lifestyles. It's not just a place to live; it's a lifestyle that aligns with the national vision to foster economic growth in Dubai.

Experience the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and prosperity in this thriving hub.

The Residential District reimagines urban spaces by providing a world-class community centered around the happiness and well-being of its residents, in alignment with Dubai South’s commitment to developing high-quality, affordable residences. It stands out with its unique design, superior build quality, and a diverse array of lifestyle amenities, all conveniently situated near the Expo City site and Al Maktoum International Airport.

SOUTH SQUARE

At the intersection of well-being and connectivity is South Square, the new chapter of wellness-centric living by Dubai South Properties. This thoughtfully planned residential community is designed to reframe everyday life around the essentials that matter most: peace of mind, access to nature, and seamless convenience. With scenic water ponds, premium wellness amenities, and lush green views, South Square is where families and individuals alike can slow down, settle in, and simply feel right at home.

SOUTHBAY

The perfect place to live and invest. South Bay is poised to become an exclusive gated community featuring premium residential units centered around a stunning 1 kilometer lagoon. It will feature 1,000 units across six clusters, each offering breathtaking views. Other world-class amenities include a waterfront promenade, a shopping mall, and health & lifestyle focused clubs dedicated for fitness and sports activities.

BEACHFRONT

Embrace a lifestyle of beauty and leisure at The Pulse Beachfront.
