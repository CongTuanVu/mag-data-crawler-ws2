Dubai's strategic location makes it a major global hub for trade and shipping. It connects fast-growing markets in Africa, India, Asia, China, and the Middle East to the entire world.

Dubai South is setting new global standards for goods handling:
Discover our Logistics District, where ultimate logistical innovation is paired with a premier infrastructure network to enhance the speed and efficiency of services and operations.
The 18-kilometre Logistics District features several zones optimised for efficient distribution. This includes the Freight Forwarding Zone, which offers direct access to Al Maktoum International Airport cargo terminals. There's also EZDubai, a dedicated e-commerce zone. Additionally, with its proximity and seamless access to Jebel Ali Port via a bonded logistics corridor, the Contract Logistics Zone enhances efficiency, especially for businesses relying heavily on sea freight.
Linked via a dedicated 200 square-kilometre, customs-bonded logistics corridor that facilitates the movement of cargo between the seaport and airport in as little as 20 minutes, the Logistics District offers customised, bespoke services to manage individual needs while also ensuring that companies enjoy a smooth experience.
