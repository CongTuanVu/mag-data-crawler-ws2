SOUTH SQUARE
At the intersection of well-being and connectivity is South Square, the new chapter of wellness-centric living by Dubai South Properties. This thoughtfully planned residential community is designed to reframe everyday life around the essentials that matter most: peace of mind, access to nature, and seamless convenience. With scenic water ponds, premium wellness amenities, and lush green views, South Square is where families and individuals alike can slow down, settle in, and simply feel right at home.

SOUTHBAY
The perfect place to live and invest.
South Bay is poised to become an exclusive gated community featuring premium residential units centered around a stunning 1 kilometer lagoon.
It will feature 1,000 units across six clusters, each offering breathtaking views. Other world-class amenities include a waterfront promenade, a shopping mall, and health & lifestyle focused clubs dedicated for fitness and sports activities.
THE PULSE
BEACHFRONT
The Pulse Beachfront is an enchanting, gated villa community. At its core lies a captivating sandy beach pool to encompass the ideal haven for living and playing in a natural environment, surrounded by private gardens, expansive green spaces, and numerous child-friendly activity centres. Embrace a lifestyle of beauty and leisure at The Pulse Beachfront.

PULSE VILLAS: Your Road to Extraordinary Life

The property offers an extraordinary lifestyle through its amenities, green parks, and designated kids play areas.
