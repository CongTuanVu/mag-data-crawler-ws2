Get to know Expo City Dubai
Find quick and informative answers to the most commonly asked questions
View all FAQs
What happens if I’m at Expo City Dubai and a Ministry of Interior (MOI) alert sounds?
Your safety and wellbeing are our highest priority. In the event that an official alert is issued, Expo City teams and security personnel will guide visitors to the nearest designated safe zone within a building, or you may proceed calmly to the nearest open building/attraction within Expo City.
Please calmly follow the instructions provided by our staff and remain in the safe area until further updates are given. Once authorities confirm it is safe to resume normal activities, operations will continue as usual.
View our safety precautions and guides
Where do I park?
If you’re driving to Expo City Dubai, you can reach here from all surrounding major roads. Follow signs for Opportunity and Sustainability parking zones, where you’ll find over 20,000 parking spaces.
Jubilee Parking zone
Al Forsan Parking zone
Sustainability Parking zone
Opportunity Parking zone
Will visitors need tickets to enter Expo City Dubai?
Expo City Dubai is an open city and access to the public areas will not require tickets. Payment and tickets will be required to enter specific attractions, or attend some events.
What are the opening hours?
Attraction timings are between 10:00 - 18:00 (Last entry 17:15)
Al Wasl Plaza is open 24/7
Jubilee Park, Al Forsan Park and the Surreal water feature are all open 24/7
