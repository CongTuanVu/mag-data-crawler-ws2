Fact Sheet: Changi Airport
31 Mar 2026
Since Singapore Changi Airport officially began commercial operations in 1981, its history has been marked by steady expansion, now reaching an annual capacity of 90 million passengers across four terminals.
High-frequency operations are a core part of Changi Airport’s history, with two runways supporting many airlines and flights every 80 seconds to major hubs like Kuala Lumpur, Bangkok, and Jakarta.
With 14 Skytrax World's Best Airport titles, a wide range of retail and dining outlets, and the 2019 opening of Jewel Changi Airport, Changi Airport continues to be the world’s most awarded airport and a well-loved destination by many.
Key Operating Numbers
100 airlines operate out of Changi Airport, serving about 170 destinations across close to 50 countries.
Changi Airport served 69.98 million passengers and handled 374,000 aircraft movements in 2025
. Over 7,300 flights operate from Changi Airport weekly, or a flight every 80 seconds.
i. Changi Airport’s busiest day in 2025 was on 20 December 2025, where over 223,000 passengers passed through its terminals.
ii. Its three busiest routes were SIN-KUL, SIN-BKK, and SIN-CGK respectively.
Changi Airport handled 2.08 million tonnes of cargo in 2025.
Changi currently has an operational capacity of 90 million passengers per annum (mppa) across four terminals.
Commercial and cargo flights operate from Changi Airport’s two runways, which are each supported by their own fire station and Airport Emergency Service (AES) team. The runways are 4000m in length.
History of Changi Airport
Prior to moving to Changi in 1981, Singapore’s international airport was located at Paya Lebar. The Paya Lebar site was converted into a military airbase after the cessation of civil aviation operations.
Changi Airport began its commercial operations in July 1981, with the opening of Terminal 1.
i. T1 was last refurbished in 2019, providing added capacity and a seamless connection for passengers to Jewel Changi Airport.
ii. Terminal 2 opened in November 1990.
T2’s latest expansion was completed in November 2023
.
iii. Terminal 3 opened in January 2008.
iv. Terminal 4 opened in October 2017, on the site of the former Budget Terminal which was demolished in September 2012.
Changi Airport was corporatised in July 2009 and has since been operated by Changi Airport Group (CAG).  In its capacity as airport operator, CAG oversees functions such as:
i. Air hub and cargo development
ii. Airport operations and management
iii. Airport concessions
iv. Aviation safety and security
v. Systems and technology maintenance.
CAG also manages Seletar Airport.
Construction of Terminal 5 is currently underway
, and the terminal is scheduled to open in the mid-2030s. Once completed, it will add another 50 mppa to Changi Airport's overall capacity.
Airport Experience
Changi Airport sits on over 1,200 hectares of land, including 870 hectares that were reclaimed from the sea.
The airport has over 360 automated gates at Departure and Arrival that are accessible to all travellers, including 64 Special Assistance Lanes (SALs) that can be used by passengers on wheelchairs, or those travelling with young children.
At the airside, Changi has close to 190 aircraft parking stands, of which over 110 are served by aerobridges.
Others
Commercial:
i. Changi Airport has around 300 retail and 140 food and beverage outlets in the transit and public areas across its terminals
People:
i. The Changi Airport community comprises 60,000 staff working across the terminals and airside.
Awards & Accolades:
i. Changi Airport is the world’s most awarded airport with over 700 accolades. This includes 14 ‘World’s Best Airport’ awards from the Skytrax World Airports Awards, the ASQ best airport awards by the Airports Council International, and the best airport in the world award by Business Traveller (UK) for 37-years straight.
Jewel Changi Airport (Jewel)
Jewel is a lifestyle destination that opened in April 2019. With over 260 retail outlets, Jewel offers visitors a multi-faceted experience that includes dining, shopping, attractions, and airport facilities.
Designed by a consortium led by world-famous architect Moshe Safdie, the mixed-use complex spans 10 storeys and covers a total gross floor area of 135,700 square metres.
At the core of the facility lies the iconic 40-metre-high indoor waterfall known as the Jewel Rain Vortex, which transforms into a mesmerising light and sound show every night.
Quick Changi Airport facts and figures
What are the busiest flight routes from Changi Airport?
The three busiest routes departing from Singapore Changi Airport are  to Kuala Lumpur (SIN-KUL), Bangkok (SIN-BKK), and Jakarta (SIN-CGK).
How many retail and dining outlets are there in Changi Airport (T1 to T4)?
There are approximately 440 outlets in total, comprising around 300 retail shops and 140 food and beverage establishments across the airport's transit and public areas.
How frequently do flights take off or land at Changi Airport?
Changi Airport handles over 7,300 flights weekly, which averages out to approximately one aircraft taking off or landing every 80 seconds.
How many times has Changi Airport been named World's Best Airport?
Changi Airport is the world’s most awarded airport, specifically clinching the ‘World’s Best Airport’ title for 14 times at the Skytrax World Airport Awards.
When did Changi Airport officially begin commercial operations?
Changi Airport officially began its commercial operations in July 1981, following its relocation from Paya Lebar and the opening of Terminal 1.
