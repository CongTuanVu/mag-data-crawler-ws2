Trip Tips & Picks

All Travel Attractions Shop & Dine SKYCITY

Unprecedented leisure and business opportunities for organisers and visitors

SKYCITY is Hong Kong’s most highly anticipated landmark. It occupies approximately 25 hectares of land and will be one of the largest integrated commercial developments in Hong Kong providing AsiaWorld-Expo guests and organisers with an eclectic selection of retail chains, dining, hotels, entertainment facilities, MICE venue and more.
 
Major highlights include the “11 SKIES” developed by New World Development – the first destination to combine wellness and wealth management with entertainment in Hong Kong.

Opening by phases, SKYCITY will add even more value to the AsiaWorld-Expo experience, offering event organisers and visitors unprecedented new opportunities for both business and leisure:

HOTEL

5,000 hotel rooms located within 10 minutes of AWE

RETAIL

Retail, dining and entertainment facilities with the gross floor area of 350,000 sqm

DINING

A wealth of culinary options for every taste and budget

ENTERTAINMENT

Establishment on latest entertainment facilities

An integral part of SKYCITY, 11 SKIES is the largest retail, dining, entertainment and commercial landmark in Hong Kong. This landmark destination will offer an eclectic choice of 800 retail shops, over 120 dining concepts, Hong Kong’s largest indoor entertainment hub offering world-class immersive experiences, as well as Grade A office towers, opening in phases between 2022 and 2025. Another highlight of SKYCITY, the newly opened Regala Skycity Hotel provides around 1,200 rooms, offering more accommodation choices.

Creating strong synergy between AsiaWorld-Expo, 11 SKIES and other components of SKYCITY, the development offers a total travel, leisure, business and event hub that will be unprecedented anywhere.
