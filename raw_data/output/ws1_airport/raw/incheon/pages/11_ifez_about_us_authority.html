"Korea's first Incheon Free Economic Zone is setting the standards for free economic zones."
Welcome to the official website of the Incheon Free Economic Zone Authority.

Since its designation in 2003 as Korea's first free economic zone, the Incheon Free Economic Zone (IFEZ), formerly a mudflat of reclaimed land, has rapidly developed into a global city housing more than 400,000 residents, 3,300 businesses, 14 international organizations including GCF, and renowned foreign universities, for the past 18 years

The Incheon Free Economic Zone is positioning itself as the center for new and upcoming industries such as new and future industries such as bio-healthcare, smart manufacturing, high-tech finance and future robots, information and communication (IT) and artificial intelligence (AI) while making great strides as a hub for distribution, logistics, tourism, and MICE.

With our outstanding infrastructure which includes the Incheon International Airport, Incheon New Port, GTX-B Line, and Airport Railroad, all of which can reach 147 cities with a population of 1 million or more within 3 hours, IFEZ will continue to actively support corporate investment and management.
We at IFEZ will provide top-notch services such as professional manpower training and preemptive administrative services, as well as create excellent living conditions in the areas of education and medical care, and develop more parks and green spaces.
The Incheon Free Economic Zone warmly welcomes investment from foreign investors and domestic companies.

Thank you very much..
