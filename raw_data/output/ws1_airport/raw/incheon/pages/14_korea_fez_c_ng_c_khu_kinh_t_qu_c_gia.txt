Korea Free Economic Zone
As centers of international business where global capital and information gather, the Korean Free Economic Zones offer the ideal management environment for multinational firms.
search
Incheon FEZ
Gateway to Korea Wide Open to the World
Incheon is turning itself into the business hub of Northeast Asia. The city has a well-established transportation network including Incheon International Airport -- which was ranked first in airport service quality by the Airports Council for 10 consecutive years -- Incheon port and the international business complex. Boasting a highly effective business environment, the IFEZ offers almost everything from logistics and medical services to education and cutting-edge industries.
search
Gangwon FEZ
Non-ferrous metals cluster and four-season Maritime tourism hub
The EFEZ has rich reserves of nonferrous metals and minerals, which are basic materials for the ultralight material industry. The zone is to host 2018 PyeongChang Winter Olympics. It has created the nation’s largest nonferrous metals cluster and invited a number of leading companies in the related business. Its future prosperity will be based on cutting-edge green materials industries, and an international logistics, using business complex, four-season maritime tourism and the winter sports hub of Asia as leverage.
search
Gyeonggi FEZ
A hub of innovative growth of new industries in the future
The Gyeonggi Free Economic Zone (GGFEZ), located at the center of the West Coast Economic Belt, consists of three districts—the Pyeongtaek-Poseung (BIX) District for high-tech, logistics, and distribution industries, the Hyeondeok District, and the Siheung-Baegot District for the research and development of unmanned land/sea/air vehicles.
search
Chungbuk FEZ
Hub of Eco-Friendly IT-Biotech Convergence Industry
The CBFEZ is located at the center of Korea, which allows fast access to inland regions and a transportation network that runs in all directions. It is Korea’s IT-biotechnology convergence industry hub, equipped with a cluster of facilities for solar energy generation, semiconductor manufacturing and biotechnology businesses. It is also turning itself into the hub of Northeast Asia’s aviation Maintenance, Repair and Operations (MRO) industry.
search
Daegu-Gyeongbuk FEZ
Transforming from manufacturing industrial complex to high-tech convergence medical industrial complex
The world's first partnership labor model(Gwangju-type automobile production plant), Creation of Korea's only national AI convergence complex and advanced industrial complex with easy accessibility, great for Business, best for Investment! Gwangju Free Economic Zone support innovative investment
search
Gwangyang Bay Area FEZ
For the Future, AI Convergence New-Industry Hub
The Gwangyang Bay Area is located on the Pacific Ocean’s main sea route. Based on this geographical advantage, the area provides business-friendly infrastructure with plentiful supplies of water and electricity. Centering on five districts themed on logistics, manufacturing, housing, tourism and other functions, the GFEZ aims to become an international trade city where new industries and cultural tourism harmonize.
search
Busan-Jinhae FEZ
Internationally Competitive Logistics Hub
The Busan-Jinhae Economic Zone (BJFEZ) is one of the best distribution hubs in Northeast Asia. BJFEZ, which handles 75% of South Korea's total freight cargo, ranked sixth in the world in terms of container handling capacity and second in transshipment. It has industrial clusters for shipbuilding, auto and machinery and is a home to electric car companies, data centers and many other next-generation digital companies.
search
Gwangju FEZ
Win-win and AI-based convergence New Industry Hub
GJFEZ is the only'AI Free Economic Zone in Korea' by setting the vision of'a new industrial hub based on win-win and artificial intelligence' and reorganizing the future food industry structure of Gwangju based on'Gwangju-type jobs' and'AI'. Operates as'.

Ulsan FEZ
Northeast Asia Energy Hub Ulsan
Ulsan is pursuing 10 projects to foster the hydrogen industry with the goal of 2030, establishing a production base for 500,000 hydrogen electric vehicles, attracting more than 200 hydrogen specialized companies, and establishing a hydrogen supply chain and charging infrastructure.

Incheon (IFEZ)
Mutual Cooperation Agreement with Hyundai Movex
2026.08.07
On Thursday, March 21, 2019, Incheon Metropolitan City and the Incheon Free Economic Zone Authority (IFEZ) signed a mutual cooperation agreement with Hyundai Movex. Under the agreement, Hyundai Movex plans to begin construction in March 2019 on a 6,684㎡ site for a logistics automation system R&amp;D center in the Incheon High-Tech Park urban high-tech industrial complex in Cheongna International City, and complete it by October 2019 for full-scale operation. Hyundai Movex is a logistics automation company established in July 2017 through the spin-off of Hyundai Elevator&rsquo;s logistics automation division and later merged with Hyundai U&amp;I, an information and communications technology affiliate of Hyundai Group. The company develops and supplies logistics automation facilities and systems.
Incheon (IFEZ)
Signing of Memorandum of Understanding with Namangan Regional Government of Uzbekistan
2026.08.07
On Friday, March 8, 2019, the Incheon Free Economic Zone Authority signed a memorandum of understanding with the Namangan Regional Government of Uzbekistan for free economic zone development and investment promotion. The memorandum included cooperation on free economic zone development, expansion of investment attraction, identification of joint cooperation projects, and revitalization of exchanges between the two institutions. Through this agreement, the authority planned to broaden its cooperative foundation with local governments in Uzbekistan and expand economic cooperation with Central Asia as well as its participation in overseas development projects.
Incheon (IFEZ)
Signing of Letter of Intent for Artificial Intelligence Collaboration with SenseTime of China
2026.08.07
On Friday, February 15, 2019, the Incheon Free Economic Zone Authority signed a letter of intent with SenseTime of China to establish a global artificial intelligence association in Songdo International City, an artificial intelligence school at Yonsei University International Campus, and a future city research center. Through this agreement, both sides planned to promote cooperation in building an artificial intelligence industry ecosystem, expanding research and education infrastructure, and attracting related companies and talent. SenseTime is a company with core artificial intelligence technologies based on facial recognition software applicable to mobile, automotive, accounting, retail, security, and robotics sectors.

The Busan-Jinhae Free Economic Zone is Northeast Asia's premier logistics hub, built
around a new port that is the world's second-largest transshipment port and seventhlargest container port. In addition to intermodal logistics created by a solid logistics

clusters for industries such as shipbuilding equipment, automotive, and machinery. It
also hosts a variety of research centers for global emerging industries, such as the bio
and medical fields.

Gwangyang Bay Area FEZ
GFEZ is developing into a functional chemicals and advanced new materials industry
cluster based on POSCO Gwangyang Steel Mill and Yeosu National Industrial Complex.
In particular, a secondary battery cluster is forming around anchor companies
specializing in secondary battery materials, such as POSCO Future M. GFEZ also boasts
stable industrial infrastructure and a business-friendly environment, such as
Gwangyang Port, which is strategically located at the center of Northeast Asia's
shipping and logistics hub. Additionally, the cultural tourism industry, which embraces
the beautiful natural scenery of the picturesque archipelago, is also developing rapidly,
helping make the area an overall more attractive place to live and do business.
