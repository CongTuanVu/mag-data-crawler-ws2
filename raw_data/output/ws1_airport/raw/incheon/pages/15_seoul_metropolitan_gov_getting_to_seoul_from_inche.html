Heat Wave Warning: Safety Tips

×
- Avoid outdoor activities during the hottest part of the day, from 2 PM to 5 PM.
- Stay indoors, drink plenty of water, and avoid caffeinated beverages and alcohol.

Getting to Seoul from Incheon Airport

Welcome to the Republic of Korea! You can get to Seoul conveniently and quickly from Gimpo Int’l Airport and Incheon Int’l Airport via various modes of transportation (e.g. airport limousine bus, express train, taxi).

Airport Limousine Bus

  • Airport limousine buses take you conveniently and quickly from Gimpo Int’l Airport and Incheon Int’l Airport to various destinations in Seoul, such as major hotels and tourist attractions.
Time
Approx. 60–80 min (varies by route, region, traffic)
Fare
₩5,000–15,000 (varies by bus)
Ticket
Designated booths in the airport (accepts cash, major credit cards, and Tmoney)

Tip!

Family discount benefit
Enjoy a special family discount benefit, which covers one free passenger fare for at least three lineal family members including at least one minor, that have expanded to all 36 bus routes including general airport buses (as of Jan. 20, 2017).

Airport bus operators

  • City Airport, Logis & Travel: www.calt.co.kr
  • Airport Limousine: www.airportlimousine.co.kr
  • Seoul Air Bus: www.seoulairbus.com
  • Korea Airport Limousine: www.klimousine.com

Airport Railroad Express

  • Airport Railroad Express (AREX) takes you conveniently and quickly from Gimpo Int’l Airport and Incheon Int’l Airport to various destinations in Seoul. You can get off directly at your desired stop or transfer to other subway lines.
Local train
Stops at all stations (8 consecutive transfer stations)
Time
59 min from Incheon Int’l Airport Terminal 1 to Seoul Station (additional 2–6 min required for some trains)66 min from Incheon Int’l Airport Terminal 2 to Seoul Station (additional 2–6 min required for some trains)
Service
Departs every 5–10 min from 5:30 AM to 12 AM (varies by subway station)
Fare
₩4,150–4,750 (one-way)
Ticket
Single-use transportation cards available at in-station vending machinesSeason pass available from in-station Information CenterPrepaid transportation cards available at convenience stores, etc.
Express train
Non-stop between Incheon Int’l Airport and Seoul Station
Time

Service

AREX Stations

Taxi

  • Taxis are readily available in front of the airport for convenient and quick rides to Seoul. There are various options such as sedan, van, and international taxi (interpretation service).
  • Regular taxi: Easy to grab with the cheapest fare
  • Deluxe taxi: Bigger and 40 to 50% more expensive than regular taxis
  • Jumbo taxi: Up to 8 seats available; same fare as deluxe taxis
  • International taxi: 20% more expensive than regular taxis; drivers can speak English, Japanese, or Chinese https://www.intltaxi.co.kr/

※ Taxi stand: directly outside Incheon Int’l Airport Arrivals Hall
