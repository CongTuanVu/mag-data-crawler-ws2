Incheon Airport’s Sustainability

Incheon Airport’s Direction for Promoting Sustainability

Since 1995, even before the construction of the terminal began, Incheon Airport has been publishing the Green Report, reflecting on environmental conservation and sustainability.

      [Environmental Management of Airport Construction]
      First Publication

      Full-scale Promotion of CSR Activities

ESG Strategy and Tasks

Laying the Groundwork for Incheon Airport’s ESG Strategy

Incheon Airport’s ESG innovation strategy incorporates the following three strategies and guidelines:
  • Incheon Airport’s Mid- to Long-Term Strategy (Vision 2030+)
    • Connecting the people and the value of life (Digital, ESG, Smart)
    • Establishing a Cultural Network (Mega Hub, Logistics, Convergence)
    • Future Paradigm (Mobility, Overseas Business, Future Airports)
  • Incheon Airport Green New Deal Strategy
    • Response to the Government’s 4th Industrial Revolution and K-New Deal
    • Establishment of a system for building a leading eco-friendly airport
    • Tasks such as Energy, Mobility, Low Carbon, and Green Transition
  • Korean Government’s Innovation Guidelines
    • Creating tangible results in social valueCreating tangible results in social value
    • Enhancing innovation-driven growth and boosting economic vitality
    • Enhancing sound institutional operations and increasing public trust
