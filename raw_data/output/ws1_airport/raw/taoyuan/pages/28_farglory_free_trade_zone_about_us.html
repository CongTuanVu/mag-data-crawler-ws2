Flying high together

Farglory inspires with a positive and innovative spirit in the world of air cargo logistics. Driven by a customer-centric culture, Farglory integrates software, hardware, and talents with international vision to offer the highest quality, customized and attentive service throughout the year.

We stay closely connected to market developments for expansion opportunities, and use a variety of resources to strengthen our cooperation with relevant operators in the industry. With a determination for teamwork, mission accomplishment and attention to detail, Farglory invests in staff training, optimization and upgrading of operation processes to enhance functional efficiency, service quality assurance and building a good brand.

Farglory focuses on delivering a diverse, customized and efficiency guaranteed service. With an integration of logistics, talents, businesses, capital, information technology, and special air cargo zone within national territory but outside customs jurisdiction, Farglory aims to build a highly efficient team, making Farglory Free Trade Zone and Farglory Logistics the ideal partners connecting your business to the global marketplace.

Created a diversified air cargo logistics service platform to export our goods for international trading

Our history

Farglory Free Trade Zone Investment Holding Co., Ltd. first began operating as Yuan-Xiang Airfreight & Warehousing in 1991. The Company was listed on the Taipei Exchange (TPEx) in 1999, and went further on to be listed on the Taiwan Stock Exchange (TWSE) in 2004. Since 2000, Farglory has actively participated in the Taoyuan Air Cargo Park Development Project promoted by the Civil Aeronautics Administration. In 2003, Farglory officially obtained the right to a 50-year development and operation under the Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT) Scheme, from where the “Farglory Free Trade Zone” took off. The Farglory Free Trade Zone stretches over 45 hectares, and is the first air cargo park cum free trade zone set “within national territory but outside customs jurisdiction” in Taiwan.

Integration of logistics, talents, businesses, capital, information technology, special air cargo zone planning set “within national territory but outside customs jurisdiction”, and through provision of services from importing raw materials, processing, and manufacturing to delivery configuration, Farglory takes care of upstream, midstream and downstream operations. Commitment to orders received in the morning being shipped in the afternoon, and attaining 100% goods delivery to the customer within 2 days (a.k.a. “1002”).

Of more than 700 free trade zones throughout the world, Farglory Free Trade Zone is the first free trade zone set “within national territory but outside customs jurisdiction” offering five integral functions. The Zone consists of air cargo terminal, warehouse operation center, domestic and foreign logistics center, value-added park and cold chain logistics warehouses to provide superb economic efficiency and competitiveness. The entire zone is expected to have an annual cargo handling capacity of 800 thousand tons, which will make Farglory the groundbreaking leader in the world of air cargo logistics.
