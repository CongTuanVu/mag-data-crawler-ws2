Joint construction of 1,924 resettlement housing units in Taoyuan Aerotropolis is expected to start in September 2024, and the City Government will set up a service center to provide professional assistance
Release Unit：Press Liaison Section
Release Date：2022-07-27
Joint construction of 1,924 resettlement housing units in Taoyuan Aerotropolis is expected to start in September 2024, and the City Government will set up a service center to provide professional assistance
Taoyuan Mayor Cheng Wen-Tsan went to Dayuan District on the morning of July 27 and attended the “Taoyuan Aerotropolis Resettlement Housing Joint Groundbreaking Ceremony.” Mayor Cheng said that during the development process of the Taoyuan Aerotropolis Project, the City Government has made the resettlement of residents and the reconstruction of their homes its top priority, and the groundbreaking ceremony of the resettlement housing signifies a new milestone for the Aerotropolis Project. The Taoyuan Aerotropolis Project adheres to two major resettlement principles: “build first, relocate later” and “resettlement in close proximity.” In addition to reserving 4,100 units of resettlement blocks (allotments) for people to build their own housing, the City Government has also contracted out the resettling housing project, and will build 1,924 houses on four sites near the MRT A18, A11, G17 and the airport area, all of which are adjacent to the MRT stations. It is believed that the housing will become a good living circle in the future. The resettlement housing is scheduled to be completed in June 2024 and handed over to the residents in September, and it is expected that the housing built by the City Government will provide quality living space for the residents.
Mayor Cheng said that for the residents to know more about the resettlement housing, the Department of Land Administration and the Office of Housing Development jointly set up a “Resettlement Housing Service Center,” and scheduled to open on September 15 this year. Dedicated staff will be available to introduce information and special offers for resettlement housing, and services such as virtual home viewing, architect self-build consultation service (online appointment), construction quality display explanation, allotment (rental) consultation, and financing consultation will be provided.
Mayor Cheng stated that the Taoyuan Aerotropolis Project has great potential for development, and the City Government has released a total of 52 hectares of nine prioritized exclusive industrial zones to attract six industries, including international logistics, aviation support, green energy industry, biotechnology, 5G smart manufacturing, and green vehicles. At present, all projects have been bid by manufacturers and companies, which represents not only the advanced investment, but also full optimism of the future development of the Taoyuan Aerotropolis Project.
Related Picture(s)
The Mayor and guests held the shovel and started the groundwork
Mayor Cheng explained that in addition to reserving 4,100 units of resettlement blocks (allotments) for people to build their own housing, the City Government has also contracted out the resettling housing project, and will build 1,924 homes to meet the settlement needs of the citizens.
The G17 site, 1 km from the MRT Green Line G17 station, is a 9-story above-ground, 2-story below-ground building with a capacity of 206 households.
The A18 site, located within 1 km from the High Speed Rail Station, has 8-10 floors above ground and 2 floors below ground, and can provide 548 households.
The resettlement houses are designed by the best architects and consultants, and are handled by the most famous brands of builders and construction companies in the country to ensure the high quality of living.
