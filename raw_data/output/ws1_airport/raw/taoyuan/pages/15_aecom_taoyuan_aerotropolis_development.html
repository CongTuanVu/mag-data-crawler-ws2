With an area of 2,302 hectares, the Taoyuan aerotropolis zone expropriation project is one of Taiwan’s largest urban development projects.

It consists of complex and extensive phases and work items. It also occupies a wide range of land which directly affects many local residents. Concerns such as their rights and benefits, resettlement, reconstruction, cultural heritage preservation and environmental protection were addressed as part of the project engagement. Achieving this required excellent and skilled project management and engineering experience.

AECOM was selected to provide high-quality services in project management and construction supervision. We help the client in carrying out the project smoothly and building a good foundation for subsequent development. Our primary services include drafting the strategy for subsequent construction procurement, construction cost estimation, schedule and phasing configuration, building a construction information exchange platform, design review and query, BIM criteria setup and management, assistance in construction tendering and construction supervision.

Awards:

  • 2025 Taoyuan City Government Engineering Safety Awards – Top Excellence Award, Government Infrastructure
  • 2021 Taoyuan City Government Public Construction Gold Quality Awards – Top Excellence Award, Civil Engineering
