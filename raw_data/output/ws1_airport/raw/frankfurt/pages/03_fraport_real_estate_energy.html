    Fraport Real Estate & Energy

    1. English
    2. Fraport Real Estate & Energy

    Fraport Real Estate & Energy offers more than 60 years of experience in real estate management at Frankfurt Airport, Europe's largest airport location. We are experts in complex and special real estate, support national and international customers in all operational and strategic issues and offer an extensive portfolio of offices, warehouses, hangar space and lounges. You can rely on our comprehensive advice and support in all leasing matters.

    Fraport Real Estate & Energy

    Fraport Real Estate & Energy is an international real estate service provider specializing in complex properties and special real estate. We support our customers in all operational and strategic issues - as a reliable partner for the entire real estate value chain.

    Development location FRA

    At Frankfurt Airport, we develop customized third-party office and logistics properties for our clients as their point of contact for building project development.

    • Mönchhof Logistics Parks
    • Gateway Gardens

         

    Attractive business park in close proximity to the airport

    The Mönchhof area was once an old refinery site that was converted into a business park. The site is one of the largest contiguous business parks yet to be developed in the Rhine-Main region.

    Further information

         

    Optimally connected

    Gateway Gardens is a joint project of the City of Frankfurt and the Gateway Gardens GmbH real estate company, in which Fraport AG, Groß & Partner Grundstücksentwicklungsgesellschaft mbH and OFB Projektentwicklung GmbH each hold a one-third stake. The real estate company is responsible for the direct development of the site and the sale of the land.

    E-Mail:  info@gateway-gardens.de
