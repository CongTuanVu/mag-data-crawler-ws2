About Us
Fraport AG is one of the leading companies in the global airport business and active at 29 airports on four continents (IPO in 2001). As an airport company, Fraport provides the full range of operational and management services relating to airside and terminal operations. The 2019 traffic figures for Frankfurt Airport are impressive: more than 1,400 aircraft took off and landed and over 6,000 tonnes of cargo were handled at FRA per day.

Management Systems
Fraport’s various management systems ensure responsible business processes across the Group. Fraport promotes a safe and healthy working environment at all Group companies, by applying a holistic approach to occupational health and safety. Processes are continuously enhanced and optimized on a Group-wide basis. A “safety first” principle applies for all airport operations at Frankfurt Airport and worldwide. Fraport’s environmental management organizes and monitors environmental protection throughout the Group. Fraport and its subsidiaries are committed to the sustainable handling of natural resources and the continuous improvement of the Group’s environmental performance.

Operations
Whether in the terminals or on the apron: Fraport organizes all airport processes responsibly and efficiently – supported by latest-generation technology. Environment and climate
Fraport AG is taking responsibility by adopting a clear and ambitious target of net zero at all of the Group’s airports by 2045. Above and beyond this, the Group’s strategies are aimed at minimizing its environmental footprint while ensuring operational excellence. The company works to protect the environment by engaging in innovative projects, promoting sustainable mobility and making use of renewable energy sources.

Sustainability Means Future Viability
Welcome to the Fraport sustainability page.
As an integral part of the global community, Fraport AG is aware of its responsibility and sees sustainability as a strategic priority within its
Fraport.2030 strategy
.
For Fraport AG, sustainability means thinking now about how we should live, work, and do business in the future. This approach is consistent with the
17 UN Sustainable Development Goals
.
Planet
The objective is clearly defined: By 2045 at the latest, Fraport AG will achieve net zero operations (Scope 1 and 2) at its homebase at FRA and all of its other fully consolidated worldwide. These climate protection measures are based on the Fraport Group's ambitious climate targets. Particular attention is given to protecting the environment and using natural resources responsibly.
People
As a top employer, the Group creates an inclusive work environment that supports personal and professional development. With its high-quality work environment, Fraport AG helps strengthen
decent working conditions and supply chains
worldwide. The communities near its locations are also to be strengthened.
Prosperity
With its products and services, the Group stands for sustainable and responsible economic growth based on innovation and efficiency.
