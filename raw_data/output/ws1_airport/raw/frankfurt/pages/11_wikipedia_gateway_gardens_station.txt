Gateway Gardens
is an underground railway station
1 kilometre (1
mi)
east of
Frankfurt Airport
in Germany. It is between
Frankfurt Stadion station
and
Frankfurt Airport regional station
on lines
S8
and
S9
of the
Rhine-Main S-Bahn
commuter network.
Construction
[
edit
]
Construction workers under station sign
Underground station box with island platform
A new tunnel was constructed under
A5 motorway
and
Bundesstraße 43
, with a new bridge over the
Main Railway
so that the existing
Frankfurt Airport loop
services could be diverted via the new station.  The station has two tracks with an island platform inside a
station box
constructed using
cut and cover
.
Opening
[
edit
]
An opening ceremony was held on 9 December 2019,
[
4
]
with public services beginning in the early hours of 15 December 2019—originally planned for 00:17,
[
5
]
but delayed until shortly after 01:30.

6
]
