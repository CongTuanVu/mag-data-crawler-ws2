A hub for innovation and growth
The
Aerotropolis
and its surrounding areas are among Australia’s fastest-growing urban regions, with a strong focus on emerging and high-tech industries.
The area is set to become a thriving, connected economy with a diverse population, skilled workforce, and vibrant lifestyle opportunities.
Investor Concierge Service
The
Bradfield Development Authority's
Investor Concierge Service is a free, confidential program that offers tailored support to help your business succeed in the Aerotropolis.
Services include:
updates on growth and opportunities in the Aerotropolis
support for business needs, including economic analysis and skills data
connections to local partners for supply chain and research collaborations
guidance on grants and financial incentives
help finding office or industrial space
coordination with government agencies and regulatory bodies
access to workforce skills development and training
assistance with visits to
Bradfield City
Let us help you unlock the potential of the Aerotropolis.
InvestorLink
InvestorLink is an
investment lead referral program
that connects private landholders in the Aerotropolis (excluding Bradfield City) with prospective investors (or occupiers), providing a transparent, efficient and equitable process for enquiry referral.
The program seeks to capture growing private sector interest in the Aerotropolis, as the opening of Western Sydney International Airport approaches.
If you would like to be involved in the program, please follow the links below:
Landholders
Open to landholders with a verified holding of at least 1,000sqm of land in the Aerotropolis who are on planning pathway.
Register to participate
Investors/Occupiers/Tenants
Open to investors/occupiers undertaking legal activity under a registered business number.

About the Aerotropolis
The Western Sydney Aerotropolis surrounds the new Western Sydney International Airport at Badgerys Creek and is set to become a thriving economic hub.
The Aerotropolis will host future-focused industries, including advanced manufacturing, technology, research, education, freight and logistics, agribusiness, and mixed-use development. At its centre will be Bradfield City – Australia’s newest city.
Quick facts
11,200 hectares of rezoned land at the centre of NSW’s road, port, and air networks.
Over $25 billion investment by the NSW and Commonwealth Governments in the airport and critical transport infrastructure.
$2 billion in 2024-25 budgets for arterial road upgrades, ensuring fast transport of goods and services.
With over $14.4 billion in planned developments, the Aerotropolis is expected to create 200,000 jobs.
Priority precincts in the Aerotropolis
The Aerotropolis is being developed in stages, creating new opportunities for industry investment. The initial focus is on 3 priority precincts:
Northern Gateway Precinct:
This precinct connects to the greater Penrith area through major transport infrastructure, providing vital links to the north.
Aerotropolis Core Precinct (including Bradfield City):
Bradfield City, located in the Aerotropolis Core Precinct, will become the new metropolitan hub of the Aerotropolis.
Agribusiness Precinct:
With direct access to the airport, this precinct supports the growth of agribusiness industries and related uses.
To learn more about these precincts, visit the
Western Sydney Aerotropolis Precinct Plan 2022 on the NSW Planning Portal
.
