Build your business in Bradfield City What is Bradfield City?

A city for global impact

Bradfield City represents a defining moment for Sydney and New South Wales. 

As Australia’s first new city in more than 100 years, it is more than a place – it represents a nationally significant investment in the industries, jobs and skills that will shape our future.

Backed by government-led infrastructure
Connected locally and globally
Built for sustainability and resilience
Access to a skilled global workforce
Advanced manufacturing innovation ecosystem
Streamlined planning pathways

M12

Where is Bradfield City?

Bradfield City sits at the heart of the Western Sydney Aerotropolis, directly adjacent to the new Western Sydney International (Nancy Bird Walton) Airport and approximately 50 kilometres west of Sydney's CBD.

City places

Australia’s first new city in 100 years, Bradfield City is built for what's next. A place where innovation and liveability grow together in boundless opportunity.

Click icons on the map to the right to explore

Enterprise precinct

The Enterprise Precinct will give businesses room to grow, with flexible, modular spaces designed for technology, manufacturing, and research next door to the AMRF and University precincts.

Read more about the Enterprise Precinct

Green loop

The 2.2 km Green Loop weaves its way through Bradfield City, offering visitors and residents an opportunity to learn about Country and First Nations culture in a green and inviting space.

AMRF precinct

Anchored by the Advanced Manufacturing Readiness Facility and semiconductor advanced packaging facility to support high-value technology, engineering, and research companies.

Read more about the AMRF Precinct

Central Park

An iconic 2-hectare main civic space welcoming visitors to Bradfield City and providing a place for people to gather, play and relax.

Read more about Central Park

Open spaces

36 hectares of green, open space, waterways and playgrounds to create a vibrant and liveable city.

Metro

The 23km Sydney Metro-Western Sydney Airport line will link travellers to Bradfield City from the new airport and the rest of Sydney’s public transport system.

Read more about Sydney Metro – Western Sydney Airport

City Precinct (First Land Release)

5.7 hectare mixed-use site that will deliver the first 1,400 homes, commercial, essential services, hotel, retail, and the University and Food & Beverage Precincts.

Read more about the City Precinct

Food and beverage

Destinational food and beverage overlooking the iconic 2-hectare Central Park.

University Precinct

A precinct dedicated to the knowledge economy, connecting research, learning and industry together to spark innovation.

Commercial Precinct

Commercial space including a city‑centre hotel designed to support business travel, industry events, delegations and international visitors just 5 minutes away from WSI airport on the metro.

Affordable housing

10% of the Bradfield City's first 1,400 homes will be designated as affordable housing for the city's first residents.

Industry sectors

Bradfield City is built around six strategic sectors and supported by three core sectors. Together they drive national capability, attract investment and create future-focused jobs.

Click on the industries to the right to explore

Advanced Manufacturing

Home to the Advanced Manufacturing Readiness Facility (AMRF), a launchpad for a high-value, precision manufacturing, robotics, automation, and new semiconductor enabled ecosystem.

Circular Economy

Bradfield City is backing the next wave of innovators and manufacturers that will fast-track emissions reduction, create new jobs and drive economic growth.

Semiconductors and other critical technologies

Bradfield City is designed to drive Australia’s economic transition and resilience, starting with the NSW Government investment in Australia’s first advanced semiconductor advanced packaging facility.

Aerospace

Bradfield City offers access to greenfield sites, skilled workforce, supply chain partners and the foundations for scaled aerospace production.

Read more about Aerospace

CleanTech

Bradfield City is a testbed for low carbon and circular economy solutions, supporting Australia’s net zero manufacturing transition.

Read more about CleanTech

Construction

With construction technologies changing rapidly, Bradfield City is set to become a national hub for innovation in Modern Methods of Construction (MMC).

Read more about Construction

Defence

Access to secure advanced manufacturing and semiconductor capabilities through the AMRF will enable stronger innovation and sovereign capability for Australia's defence sector.

Read more about Defence

Education and research

Presence and partnerships with major universities will form the foundation of the knowledge economy in Bradfield City.

Read more about Education and research

Freight and logistics

Direct connections to global and national markets through growing Western Sydney Aerotropolis road, rail, and air freight systems create a proximity advantage.

Read more about Freight and logistics
