About the Bradfield Development Authority
The Bradfield Development Authority is building Western Sydney's future through Bradfield City and attracting investment to the Aerotropolis.

About us
We work with governments, businesses, and the community to make the most of the Western Sydney International (Nancy-Bird Walton) Airport.
We do this by leading the creation of Australia's newest city, attracting investment to the region, and growing industry.
Learn more about our projects
.
Leading delivery of the 114-hectare Bradfield City precinct.
Growing industry with the Advanced Manufacturing Readiness Facility (AMRF).
Attracting investment to the Aerotropolis region.
