Delivering Bradfield City
We’re creating Bradfield City, a vibrant hub for innovation, sustainability, and economic growth. With world-class infrastructure and cutting-edge industries, it will be a great place to work, invest, and connect.
Learn how Bradfield City will shape Western Sydney’s future.

Investing in the Western Sydney Aerotropolis
The Western Sydney Aerotropolis is growing into a hub for industry and innovation. With world-class infrastructure and a strong business network, it’s the perfect place to invest and succeed.
Explore how investing in the Aerotropolis creates opportunities.

Advanced Manufacturing Readiness Facility (AMRF)
The AMRF helps businesses innovate, grow, and compete globally. With cutting-edge technology, expert support, and training, it speeds up development and reduces risk.
Find out how the AMRF supports industry success.
